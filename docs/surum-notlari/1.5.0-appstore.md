This release adds two new modules: an emergency button and smart home.

EMERGENCY BUTTON
There is an SOS button in the top corner of every screen. One tap calls for help; you choose the kind of emergency — at home, a security incident, or a site-wide announcement.

If you pressed it by mistake, you can cancel within 5 seconds; nothing is sent to anyone until that window closes.

Once the alarm goes out, a tracking screen opens: you see who it reached, who has seen it, who said "on my way", and how long the response took. The alarm is closed with a note.

The security team and management follow open alarms in a list. Emergencies in other units are not shown to residents.

Important: this system does not replace your national emergency number. In a life-threatening situation call the official emergency line first. This warning also appears next to the button.

SMART HOME
You can control your unit's smart devices from the app: lights, sockets, locks, blinds, thermostat. You only see the devices in your own unit — your neighbour's devices never appear in your list.

When a water leak or smoke is detected you get a notification and a predefined action runs by itself: on a leak, for example, the main valve closes.

Management decides which sections are enabled (lighting, climate, locks, blinds, leak, fire, energy, meters, scenarios); disabled sections appear on nobody's screen.

INTERCOM (MANAGEMENT)
The door panel on site can be connected to the app: connection test, doorbell and door release. A text announcement can be pushed to the panel screen.

CONNECTION HEALTH (MANAGEMENT)
Connections that talk to the outside world (payments, messaging, cameras, intercom, smart home) are checked regularly. When one drops, management is notified; a single screen shows which connection has been silent and since when.

SHIFTS AND PATROLS
Days in shift and patrol plans are now picked from a calendar. A patrol plan can say which weekdays it runs. The shift card on the home screen shows who is on duty right now.

CARRIED OVER FROM 1.4
This release also contains what was prepared in 1.4.x but never published:

• Task steps: a task can be split into steps, each completed on its own with its own photo and note. You can require them to be done in order.
• Polls rewritten: create a poll in the app, add an image and dates, choose who receives it; results as a chart.
• Anonymous polls: votes are collected without identities and who voted for what is not recorded.
• Icons in screen headers now say what they do.
• Fixes to the phone number field.
• The app reminds you at startup when a new version is available.
