## en (797 karakter)\n\n```\nEmail delivery
Invitations, verification codes and notifications now go through a new provider. More importantly: an email that doesn't arrive is now visible. If the address is wrong or the mailbox is full, the management panel says "bounced" — previously it said "sent" and the message quietly disappeared.

Phone field
The country code is now picked from a list instead of typed. The correct number of digits is applied per country, and extra digits are refused. Numbers are shown as (+90) 541 922 23 88.

Email field
It warns while you type, so an incomplete or invalid address is caught before it is saved.

Shift plan
Days are picked from a calendar, and different days can get different hours. Before saving, you see how many shifts will be created.

Plus small fixes and speed improvements.\n```\n
