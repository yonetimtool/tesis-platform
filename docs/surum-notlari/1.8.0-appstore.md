## en — English (1972 karakter)

```
EMERGENCY ALERTS NOW TELL YOU WHAT TO DO
For earthquake, fire, gas leak and evacuation alerts, the top of the screen shows the type of alert and below it, step by step, what to do. The instructions are summarised from Turkey's disaster management (AFAD) and fire service guidelines. Answer "I am safe" or "I need help"; security and management can see which units are safe and who is waiting for help.

For medical and security calls, the security team sees who needs help and in which unit right in the notification.

NOTIFICATIONS
Emergency notifications also come through in Focus mode. The "SOS alarm settings" section in Settings shows how the alarm will ring on your phone.

DRILLS
Management can start an earthquake, fire, gas or evacuation drill, or schedule one for a later date. Drill notifications and screens start with "DRILL" everywhere. Afterwards, a report shows how many people were reached and who answered.

VISITOR APPROVAL
When someone at the gate says you are expecting them, security can ask you. Approve or refuse from the notification; your answer reaches security immediately.

VOICE MESSAGES
Security can leave a short voice message for your unit. You can listen to or delete messages; they are deleted automatically after 7 days.

PHONE PERMISSION
If you turn on "Management may call me on this number" in Settings, security may call you when they cannot reach your unit any other way. Your number does not appear in any list.

SECURITY CHIEF
Security chiefs can now register and sign in from the app; the security chief account is used in the app. Security chiefs can also watch past camera recordings in the app. Managers can add and manage security chiefs from the "Field staff" screen in the app.

PHONE NUMBERS
Every phone number field lets you pick the country code from a list and formats the number for that country; foreign numbers are accepted. On the sign-in screen, the country code box appears as soon as you start typing a number.
```
